Questions

Is NULLUME free?

Yes. The free plan includes 3 readings a month. Seeker and Oracle add unlimited readings, audio, PDF and more.

Are the readers real people?

No. Every NULLUME reader is an AI character with a written life and the same reading method.

Can I use my own deck?

Yes, on every plan. Type the cards you drew, or photograph your spread.

Does tarot predict the future?

No. A NULLUME reading offers perspective on your question, not a prediction.
