Is AI tarot accurate? How a NULLUME reading is built

The NULLUME Journal21 Oct 20267 min read

An AI tarot reading can't be "accurate" about the future, because tarot doesn't predict it. What it can be is accurate to the cards: reading every card that was drawn, in its position, with its real imagery, and naming what the cards do together. That's the standard NULLUME holds itself to.

What "accurate" can honestly mean

Tarot is a tool for reflection. A good reading is faithful to the cards on the table and useful to the person asking. It is not a forecast.

Four rules every NULLUME reader follows

  1. Read only the cards you were given. Never invent a card.
  2. Read every card, in its position — none skimmed.
  3. Name the pairings and the contradictions.
  4. Commit to one conclusion, kindly, and say what it's based on.

Who are the readers?

AI characters. Each has a written life and a voice, on top of the same method. We say so plainly, everywhere.

What a reading is not

Not medical, legal or financial advice. Not a prediction. Perspective, not prediction.
