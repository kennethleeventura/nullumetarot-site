A sample reading: five cards, one question.

1 · The shape of the table

You asked about a job in Denver, and you asked it like a yes-or-no question. The table answers a different one. Five cards, two of them Major Arcana, and the Tower sits right in the middle. Before anything else: there is not a single Wand here.

2 · Every card

The Tower, in the present. Something has already come down. The figures are falling, but look at the crown: it was knocked off by the light, not by the people. Whatever is ending in your current job, it ended before this question.

The Queen of Swords, behind you. She has made decisions alone and made them well. Her hand is open. You know how to cut cleanly; you have done it before.

3 · Every pairing

Reinforces · The Tower · The Star · The collapse, then the clearing

The Tower and the Star are numbered sixteen and seventeen. One follows the other. The Star is what the sky looks like once the tower is gone: quiet, wide, and honest.

Contradicts · Ace of Cups · Five of Pentacles · Full and empty

What you were recently offered is emotional abundance; what sits at the root is practical scarcity. Both are true. The contradiction isn't an error in the reading — it is the reading.

“Nothing on this table moves until you name what happened.”

4 · What isn't on the table

No Wands. Wands are drive, appetite, the wanting itself. Every other suit showed up to talk about Denver. Desire didn't. Before you decide, ask whether you want the job, or want to be gone.

5 · The through-line

The leaving has already happened inside you. Denver can be the clearing or just a new tower. Take the offer seriously, ask for the numbers in writing, and give yourself one week where nothing has to be decided.

A question to keepWhat would you do if slow were allowed?
