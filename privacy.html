Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · 2882 LLC

This policy explains what 2882 LLC collects when you use NULLUME, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, name if you give one, and password (stored hashed).
  • Reading content: your questions, card lists, spread photos, notes, saved readings and readers you build.
  • Purchases: plan, purchase history and, for printed decks, your shipping name and address. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we receive only limited details such as card brand and last four digits.
  • Waitlist: the email address you enter on this page.
  • Usage and device data: pages viewed, features used, browser type and approximate location from your IP address, used to run and improve the service.

How we use it

  • To create your readings, narration, Keepsake files and decks.
  • To remember past readings when you ask your reader to.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud and meet tax and legal obligations.
  • To email you about your account, receipts, renewals and, if you opted in, NULLUME news. You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run NULLUME, under contracts that limit their use of it: Stripe (payments), our AI and text-to-speech providers (to generate readings and narration), our hosting and database providers, our email provider, and our print and shipping partner for deck orders. We may disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Cookies

We use cookies needed to keep you signed in and to secure checkout, plus basic analytics to understand which pages are used. You can block cookies in your browser; some features may then stop working.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and readings while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your readings and profile within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Your rights, including California residents

You can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal information, and to opt out of marketing. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right not to be discriminated against for using them. Email kennethleeventura@gmail.com from the address on your account; we respond within 45 days.

Children

NULLUME is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you think your account has been compromised.

Changes

We'll post any update here with a new effective date, and email you if the change is significant.
