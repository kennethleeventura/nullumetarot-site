No Major Arcana in a tarot spread: what it means.

The Major Arcana are the big forces and turning points. When none appear, the situation is in your hands: everyday choices, not fate or timing, will decide it.

Why a missing group matters

Twenty-two of the 78 cards are Major Arcana, so a spread without any is common. It isn't a lesser reading. It tells you where the power sits: with you and the people around you.

What no Major Arcana can point to

  • A practical problem that practical steps will solve
  • Less at stake than it feels like right now
  • Freedom to choose, without waiting for a sign

What to ask

“What is one ordinary thing I can do about this today?”
