10 cards · Seeker and Oracle

The Celtic Cross tarot spread: every position explained.

Ten positions: a cross at the centre for the situation, and a staff of four for you and what's around you. Tap a position to see what it asks.

Position 1 The present The heart of the matter: what is actually happening right now.

How the positions talk to each other

1 against 2: what's happening and what's crossing it. The crossing card lies at 90° and can be lifted aside.

3 against 5: what's underneath against what you're reaching for. When they contradict, that gap is often the reading.

4 against 6: what's leaving and what's arriving.

7 against 10: where you stand now, and where this is heading if nothing changes.

When to use it

For a situation with many moving parts — a relationship, a career turn — when a three-card spread feels too thin. For a quick daily look, choose a single card.
