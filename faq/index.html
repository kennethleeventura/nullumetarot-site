Readings

What happens in a NULLUME reading?

Your reader reads every card in its position, names the pairings and contradictions, reads what is missing, and ends with one conclusion and a question to keep.

How long is a reading?

From a few minutes for a single card to about a quarter of an hour for a Deep Dive.

What if I don’t have a question?

Leave the focus blank. Your reader reads blind and lets the spread name its own subject.

Does tarot predict the future?

No. A NULLUME reading offers perspective on your question, not a prediction.

Readers

Are the readers real people?

No. Every NULLUME reader is an AI character with a written life, a voice and the same reading method.

Can I build my own reader?

Yes. Name, pronouns, style, specialties, background and an AI portrait. Free accounts include one.

Does my reader remember me?

Yes. Your reader can see short summaries of your recent readings, so recurring cards and unfinished threads come up.

Decks and cards

Can I use my own deck?

Yes, on every plan. Type the cards you drew, or photograph your spread.

How does photo reading work?

Take one photo of your spread from above. Every card, position and reversal is identified, and you confirm the list before the reading.

Which decks are included?

Rider–Waite–Smith and Tarot de Marseille on every plan; Sola Busca, Visconti–Sforza and Tarot de Viéville on Seeker and Oracle.

Plans

Is NULLUME free?

Yes. The free plan includes 3 readings a month.

What’s a Keepsake?

The designed PDF and narrated audio of one reading, $2.82 on the free plan. Included on paid plans.

How do I cancel?

From your billing page, any time. Your readings, cards and readers are kept.

Privacy

Who can see my readings?

Only you, unless you choose to share one.

Can I delete my readings and my account?

Yes, from your account settings.
