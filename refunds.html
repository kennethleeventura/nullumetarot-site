Refunds, Cancellation & Shipping

Effective October 23, 2026 · 2882 LLC

This policy covers subscriptions, one-time digital purchases and printed decks bought from NULLUME, operated by 2882 LLC.

Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel any time from your billing page or by emailing support@nullumetarot.com. No fees and no call required.
  • Cancellation stops the next charge. You keep paid access until the end of the month or year you already paid for, then your account moves to the Free plan. Your readings and readers are kept.

Subscription refunds

  • First charge: if you're not happy, email us within 7 days of your first paid charge on a plan and we'll refund it in full.
  • Yearly plans: if you cancel within 14 days of a yearly renewal charge, we'll refund that renewal in full.
  • Otherwise, monthly and yearly charges aren't refunded for partial periods, except where the law requires it.

Digital purchases (Keepsake, Deep Dive, extra portrait)

These are delivered instantly. If one fails to generate, arrives incomplete, or you were charged twice, email us within 30 days and we'll refund it or deliver it again, whichever you prefer. Because they are delivered immediately, completed digital purchases are otherwise not refundable.

Printed decks

  • Made to order. Each deck is printed for you, so we can't accept returns for change of mind, including personalized decks.
  • Damaged, misprinted or wrong item: email us within 30 days of delivery with a photo, and we'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping.
  • Lost in transit: if tracking shows no delivery 10 business days after the expected date, we'll reship or refund.
  • Cancelling a deck order: you can cancel for a full refund until the deck goes to print; we'll tell you when it does.

Shipping

  • We ship printed decks to addresses in the United States. Shipping is $8.82 per order, free on the Seeker plan.
  • Production and delivery estimates are shown at checkout before you pay, and you'll get a tracking number by email when your order ships.
  • Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Billing questions

Email support@nullumetarot.com with the email address on your account and the date of the charge. We reply within 2 business days.
