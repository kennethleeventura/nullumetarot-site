How it reads

How a NULLUME reading is built.

One written method, followed by every reader on every reading. The persona changes the voice. It never changes the depth.

Read a sample Begin a reading — free
The crossing card lies at 90° over the card it crosses.
  1. 1

    Before the cards

    Your reader starts with how you asked, not with generic mysticism. Give no focus, and the spread names its own subject.

  2. 2

    The shape of the table

    The spread is named, and the first thing noticed: a dominant suit, three Major Arcana in five cards, a suit that's missing.

  3. 3

    Every card

    Its image, its position and your question. Reversals read as blocked, delayed or overdone — never simply "the opposite". Number and element included.

  4. 4

    Every pairing

    Repeated numbers, cards that answer or contradict each other, elemental friction. Each relationship is drawn as a mark and written as words.

    Reinforces— two cards push the same way
    Complements— each supplies what the other lacks
    Contradicts— two truths pull apart
  5. 5

    What isn't on the table

    Missing suits, numbers and court cards are read as seriously as what's there. The absence is often the loudest thing on the table.

  6. 6

    The through-line

    One committed conclusion, kind and clear, practical steps, and a question to keep.

    “What would you do if slow were allowed?”

  7. 7

    Who is reading

    Your readers are AI characters. Each is a written persona — a voice, a style, specialties — on top of the same method. They read only the cards you give them. They never invent a card.

    Meet the readers →
  8. 8

    What a reading is not

    Not a prediction. Not medical, legal or financial advice. Perspective, not prediction.
