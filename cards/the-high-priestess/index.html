Rider–Waite–Smith · Pamela Colman Smith, 1909
Major Arcana · II

The High Priestess tarot card meaning

Upright, the High Priestess is knowledge you already hold but haven't said out loud. Reversed, it is ignoring that knowing, or keeping a secret that has started to cost you.

The image

A woman sits between a black pillar marked B and a white pillar marked J. A crescent moon lies at her feet; she holds a scroll marked TORA, half hidden in her robe. Behind her hangs a veil printed with pomegranates, and a glimpse of water beyond it.

Upright

Wait, watch, and trust the part of you that already knows. Not every answer comes from more information.

Reversed

Second-guessing a clear instinct, or hiding something from yourself because looking at it would change things.

In love, work, money and yourself

In love

There's more going on beneath the surface, in them or in you.

In work

Hold your cards. Listen more than you speak this week.

In money

Don't act on rumours. Quiet research beats a quick move.

In yourself

Your intuition has been right before. Give it a hearing.
