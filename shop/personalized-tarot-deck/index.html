About the personalized deck

This is a full 78-card Rider–Waite–Smith tarot deck with Pamela Colman Smith's 1909 art on the faces and your name on every card back, in the back colour you choose: umber, vellum, verdigris or ember. It comes in a matching box. Each deck is printed one at a time in the USA and checked card by card before it ships.

You approve a proof before your deck is printed. Add a rigid box, a guidebook or gift wrap if you like. Shipping is $8.82 in the US and free for Seeker members; Oracle members save 15% on printed decks.
