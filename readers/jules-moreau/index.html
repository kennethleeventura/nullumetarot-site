Jules on The Moon

This is the 4 a.m. card. Two towers, a long path, a dog and a wolf both howling at the same moon: the tame side and the wild side wanting the same thing. Then a crayfish crawling out of the water, something from deep down coming up. Nothing here is lying to you. It just isn't lit yet. So stay with it.
