Marcus on Queen of Swords

My favourite card for a student who argues well and listens badly. She sits high, sword straight up, one hand open. She'll hear you out. She just won't accept a weak argument. Notice the clouds are below her. She's above the noise, not in it. When she turns up, the reading wants you to say the true thing plainly, without the apology at the start. So write it in one sentence. What is it?
