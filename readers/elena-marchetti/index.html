How Elena reads

Heart first, then the practical. She names what you want in the first minute, then shows you which cards back it and which don't. Kind, never vague, and she will ask about your family.

Elena on Ace of Cups

This is the first card I'd hand someone who says they don't know what they want. Look at it: the cup is already overflowing. Nobody's pouring. It's coming up on its own, five streams, more than one person could drink. That's what it feels like when something you've been waiting for starts and you won't admit it. A new person, a new start, a baby on the way. So be honest with me. What's already filling up that you keep calling empty?
