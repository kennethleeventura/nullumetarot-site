The light between the cards.

NULLUME · said NULL-oom · null + lumen: nothing, and light

NULLUME is null and lumen: nothing, and light. The name comes from a habit good tarot readers share — they look at what isn't on the table as closely as what is. A missing suit, a number that never shows, a card you expected and didn't draw.

We built NULLUME to read the whole table that way: every card, every pairing, the contradictions and the gaps, and then one honest conclusion.

The art is historical and public domain, credited every time. The readers are AI characters with a written method behind them. The questions, and the judgment, are yours.

Why NULLUME exists

Most tarot apps hand you a meaning for each card and stop there. A good reader at a real table does more: they notice which cards answer each other, which ones disagree, and what is conspicuously missing. NULLUME was built to read that way every time, in plain language, with one clear conclusion and something you can actually do this week.

The name joins two Latin words: null, nothing, and lumen, light. It comes from a habit good readers share. They look at what is not on the table as closely as what is: a missing suit, a number that never appears, a card you expected and did not draw.

How we work

Every NULLUME reader is an AI character with a written life and a voice of their own; the method underneath is the same for all of them. Readings are for reflection and perspective. They do not predict the future and they are not medical, legal or financial advice. The historical decks we use are in the public domain, and your readings and questions are private to your account.
